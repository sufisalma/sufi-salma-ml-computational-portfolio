## 📂 Featured Projects

### 1. Advanced Regression & Feature Engineering (Kaggle Housing Prices)
- **Objective:** Built and optimized regression models to forecast complex, non-linear housing datasets, achieving a Top 15% ranking on the Kaggle leaderboard.
- **Key Actions:** Engineered domain-specific interaction features, handled skewed distributions via log-transformation, applied L2 Regularization (Ridge), and systematically tuned hyperparameters to balance the bias-variance tradeoff.
- **📓 [View Notebook](https://github.com/sufisalma/sufi-salma-ml-computational-portfolio/blob/main/01_Housing%20Price%20Prediction_ML.ipynb)**

### 2. Predictive Classification & Data Preprocessing (Income Dataset)
- **Objective:** Developed k-NN classification pipelines to analyze multidimensional demographic datasets.
- **Key Actions:** Implemented custom "Smart Binarization" and MinMax scaling to prevent numerical dominance in distance metrics. Analyzed model fairness, bias extrapolation, and the impact of high-dimensional sparse spaces on algorithmic performance.
- **📓 [View Notebook](https://github.com/sufisalma/sufi-salma-ml-computational-portfolio/blob/main/02_Income_kNN_Preprocessing.ipynb)**

### 3. Monte Carlo Transport & Uncertainty Quantification (UQ)
- **Objective:** Modeled stochastic particle transport and quantified statistical uncertainties in shielding simulations.
- **Key Actions:** Implemented analog and implicit capture (survival biasing) Monte Carlo methods. Applied variance reduction techniques to optimize the Figure of Merit (FOM). Utilized **Numba JIT compilation** to accelerate computational runtime by over 60%.
- **Relevance:** These computational methods for modeling complex, noisy systems and quantifying statistical uncertainty are directly transferable to Earth system modeling, climate dynamics, and extreme event simulations.
- **📓 [View Notebook](https://github.com/sufisalma/sufi-salma-ml-computational-portfolio/blob/main/03_MonteCarlo_Transport_UQ.ipynb)**
